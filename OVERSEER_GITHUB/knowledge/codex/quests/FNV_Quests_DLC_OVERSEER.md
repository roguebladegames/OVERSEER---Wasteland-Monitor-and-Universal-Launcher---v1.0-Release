# OVERSEER Quest Codex — DLC Operations

Original briefings for the four story expansions plus courier packs. Accuracy-focused objectives.

---

# Dead Money (Sierra Madre)

## Sierra Madre Grand Opening (arrival / collar)
**Hook:** Radio lure into a trapped villa casino hell.  
**Objectives:**
1. Survive arrival in the Villa.  
2. Receive the explosive collar rules (speakers, proximity, cloud).  
3. Accept Elijah’s forced heist.  
**Notes:** Cloud, holograms, and speakers are environmental killers.

## Find Collar Crew (assemble specialists)
**Hook:** Three specialists are collared into cooperation.  
**Objectives:**
1. Locate and free **Dog/God** (nightkin muscle; dual personality).  
2. Locate and free **Dean Domino** (ghoul crooner/conman — doors, social angles).  
3. Locate and free **Christine Royce** (Brotherhood hunter — codes, radios, medical trauma).  
4. Return them to the staging plan under collar threat.  
**Notes:** Roles are complementary; killing a crewmate early bricks paths.

## Open the Sierra Madre Casino (villa gauntlet)
**Objectives:**
1. Clear or sneak toxic cloud routes.  
2. Disable/avoid speaker detonators.  
3. Crack villa obstacles with each specialist’s skillset.  
4. Reach the casino lobby systems.  

## Gala Event
**Objectives:**
1. Trigger the pre-war gala sequence.  
2. Use the chaos to push deeper into casino security.  

## Heist / vault ending
**Objectives:**
1. Station crew as the heist requires.  
2. Bypass final vault protections.  
3. Decide escape vs gold greed vs who gets collared/killed.  
**Notes:** End states swing hard on loyalty and greed. Elijah is the true architect antagonist.

# Honest Hearts (Zion)

## Arrival in Zion / Happy Trails collapse
**Hook:** Caravan into Zion collapses into tribal war.  
**Objectives:**
1. Survive the White Legs ambush.  
2. Meet **Joshua Graham** (Dead Horses) and/or **Daniel** (Sorrows missionary).  
3. Choose which moral map you trust.  

## Early Zion survival
**Objectives:**
1. Secure food, maps, and safe camps.  
2. Optional: help refugees and side tablets/lore caches.  

## Gather support against the White Legs
**Objectives:**
1. Scout White Legs war parties.  
2. Complete tribal trials/respect tasks for Dead Horses and/or Sorrows.  
3. Recruit temporary guides (**Follows-Chalk**, **Waking Cloud**) as available.  

## Zion finale
**Objectives:**
1. Commit to Graham’s warpath or Daniel’s evacuate/protect path (or scripted hybrids your checks allow).  
2. Confront **Salt-Upon-Wounds**.  
3. Lock Zion’s cultural future.  
**Notes:** Crusade vs protective isolation is the philosophical split — not NCR vs Legion dam politics.

# Old World Blues (Big MT)

## Welcome to the Big Empty / kidnapping
**Hook:** Think Tank steals your spine (and more) for research.  
**Objectives:**
1. Wake in Big MT / meet the Think Tank.  
2. Understand implant theft.  
3. Agree to work for organ return.  

## Field missions (X-labs pattern)
**Objectives (repeat across districts):**
1. Travel to named research sites (X-2, X-8, X-13, Higgs, etc. as assigned).  
2. Retrieve tech or clear horrors.  
3. Return data to Klein’s Think Tank.  

## Confront Mobius / Forbidden Zone
**Objectives:**
1. Learn why Big MT loops on itself.  
2. Deal with **Dr. Mobius** in the Forbidden Zone.  
3. Choose Think Tank lobotomy-of-conscience vs allowing their continued “science.”  

## Body restoration finale
**Objectives:**
1. Reclaim remaining biology.  
2. Settle Think Tank fate.  
3. Exit to the Mojave with MT implants/toys.  

### Sink & gadget side objectives
**Objectives:** Restore Sink personalities; unlock crafting/stealth suit/sonic tools via scavenges.

# Lonesome Road (The Divide)

## The Package / enter the Divide
**Hook:** Another courier’s trail through missile-scarred canyons.  
**Objectives:**
1. Enter the Divide from the Mojave gate.  
2. Follow Ulysses’s messages.  
3. Survive Marked Men, Tunnelers, and storms.  

## Hopeville → Ashton progress
**Objectives:**
1. Restore power/pathing through blocked war architecture.  
2. Advance ED-E’s wartime identity/upgrades.  
3. Push toward the silo/temple spine.  

## The Courier and Ulysses (finale)
**Objectives:**
1. Reach Ulysses’s stage.  
2. Resolve via speech or combat.  
3. Decide missile targets — or refuse the launch.  
**Notes:** Ending hits NCR/Legion logistics harder than Strip casino ownership.

## ED-E Divide memories
**Objectives:** Complete eyebot memory beats before the end for full context and upgrades.  
**Notes:** Related to base ED-E’s line, but this instance is Divide wartime truth.

# DLC sequencing tips (OVERSEER)
- Soft level bands: Dead Money mid; Honest Hearts mid; OWB mid-high; Lonesome Road high.  
- Play order is preference; each assumes you can already fight, sneak, or talk.  
- Unique gear carried in may be inaccessible until that DLC exits.  
- Dead Money collars punish loud play; Zion rewards exploration; Big MT rewards scav completionism; Divide rewards endurance.
