# OVERSEER Quest Codex — Main Spine (Base Game)

Original operational briefings. Objectives rewritten for OVERSEER; not copied from guides or wikis.

Format per entry:
- **Hook:** Why it exists  
- **Objectives:** What success looks like (branch-aware, spoiler-light)  
- **Notes:** Path forks / fail states

---

## Ain't That a Kick in the Head
**Hook:** Tutorial wake-up after Benny’s double-cross in the Goodsprings cemetery.  
**Giver / place:** Doc Mitchell’s house, Goodsprings.  
**Objectives:**
1. Wake in Doc Mitchell’s care after being shot and buried.  
2. Complete character setup (SPECIAL, traits, skills, optional tag tutorial).  
3. Leave the house ready for the wastes.  
**Notes:** Frames the hunt for the checkered-suit man (Benny) and the Platinum Chip.

## Back in the Saddle
**Hook:** Doc points you at the town sharpshooter for basic field training.  
**Giver / place:** Sunny Smiles, Goodsprings.  
**Objectives:**
1. Speak with Sunny Smiles (not Trudy, not a random NCR Ranger).  
2. Complete her marksmanship lesson (tin cans / practice shots as directed).  
3. Finish her assigned local pest work if she continues the lesson into geckos on this step in your build — otherwise geckos are the next quest.  
**Notes:** Onboarding only. Sunny is Goodsprings-only; not a companion; never Novac.

## By a Campfire on the Trail
**Hook:** Sunny takes you gecko hunting and points you after Benny.  
**Giver / place:** Sunny Smiles, north of Goodsprings.  
**Objectives:**
1. Meet Sunny for the gecko hunt (Cheyenne may come).  
2. Clear geckos and finish the field lesson.  
3. Hear that the shooter headed toward Primm / the southern highway.  
**Notes:** Still Sunny Smiles — not Boone, not Ranger Andy.

## They Went That-A-Way
**Hook:** Track Benny and the Platinum Chip across early Mojave stops.  
**Giver / place:** Starts after Sunny’s tip; advances by location.  
**Objectives:**
1. Reach **Primm** — deal with Powder Ganger chaos; rescue/speak to Deputy Beagle (and related town threads) for Benny intel.  
2. Reach **Nipton** — witness Legion aftermath (Boxcars / Vulpes possible); learn the trail continues.  
3. Reach **Novac** — interview locals (e.g. Manny) for Benny / Tops direction.  
4. Optional parallel: **Boulder City** Khan/NCR fallout (Jessup) can feed the same manhunt story.  
5. Confirm the trail ends at the **New Vegas Strip / The Tops**.  
**Notes:** Side content can interleave freely. Quest completes as you assemble the trail to Vegas, not as a single building clear.

## Ring-a-Ding-Ding!
**Hook:** Confront Benny inside Chairmen territory on the Strip.  
**Giver / place:** The Tops; requires Strip access first.  
**Objectives:**
1. Enter the Strip (classically ~2,000 caps credit check, or enough fame, or other legitimate entry).  
2. Enter The Tops and navigate Chairmen security (Swank can help depending on approach).  
3. Confront Benny — kill, spare, speech paths, or allow him to flee (including Fort outcomes in some branches).  
4. Recover the **Platinum Chip** when the plot state allows.  
**Notes:** Major branch into House, Yes Man/Wild Card, NCR, or Legion. Save before the Benny resolution.

## Mr. House path — The House Always Wins (I–VIII)

**Hook:** Work for the Lucky 38 autocrat after the chip is in play.  
**Giver / place:** Mr. House, Lucky 38 penthouse.  
**Typical objective chain (official part labels):**
1. **The House Always Wins I** — Gain an audience; deliver/install the Platinum Chip relationship with House.  
2. **The House Always Wins II** — Activate House’s control network at the **El Dorado Dry Lake weather station** (south of HELIOS One).  
3. **The House Always Wins III** — Neutralize the Mojave **Brotherhood of Steel** as a threat to House’s plan (destroy or otherwise remove them from his threat board).  
4. **The House Always Wins IV** — Upgrade the Securitron army at the **Securitron vault** (underground bunker tied to Fortification Hill / chip upgrade — Mk II army).  
5. **The House Always Wins V–VII** — Further Strip/Mojave control tasks House assigns (family crises, political cleanup, dam prep as your game state requires).  
6. **The House Always Wins VIII / dam package** — Final alignment so House can contest Hoover Dam.  
**Finale container:** **All or Nothing** (House victory at the dam).  
**Notes:** Do not invent non-game codenames (no “Osiris”). Later House steps conflict with full NCR occupation and Legion victory paths. Killing House early collapses this line into Wild Card or other banners.

## Yes Man / Wild Card path

**Hook:** Cut House out; run New Vegas through Yes Man.  
**Giver / place:** Yes Man (secured via Benny’s Tops systems / related plot); Lucky 38 becomes your deck.  
**Official Wild Card containers:**
1. **Wild Card: Ace in the Hole** — Secure Yes Man as your interface.  
2. **Wild Card: Change in Management** — Remove House’s exclusive control (House dies or is otherwise out).  
3. **Wild Card: You and What Army?** — Claim/upgrade the Securitron army for yourself.  
4. **Wild Card: Side Bets** — Stabilize or neutralize regional powers (Boomers, Brotherhood, Khans, etc. as checklist items).  
5. **Wild Card: Finishing Touches** — Last prep before the dam.  
**Finale container:** **No Gods, No Masters**.  
**Notes:** Highest sandbox freedom; most self-directed prep. Side Bets are not optional fluff if you want an easy dam.

## NCR path — For the Republic (and related)

**Hook:** Become the Republic’s troubleshooter in the Mojave.  
**Giver / place:** Builds through Camp McCarran, NCR Embassy (Ambassador Crocker), officers chain.  
**Objectives (pattern):**
1. Earn positive NCR reputation via base and field work (Fiends, logistics, town support).  
2. Progress **For the Republic, Part 2** once embassy/military trust is high enough.  
3. Handle McCarran crises (**I Put a Spell on You**, Fiend pressure, monorail security).  
4. Coordinate east-front / dam prep (Forlorn Hope, allies, Kimball speech security as assigned).  
5. Commit via **Don’t Tread on the Bear!** when NCR demands exclusive loyalty (locks other endgame banners).  
**Finale container:** **Eureka!**  
**Notes:** Occupation optics matter. Boone and NCR officers react to Legion-friendly acts.

### Important NCR-linked main-ish ops (not all are “For the Republic” steps)
- **Don’t Tread on the Bear!** — Late NCR lock-in; hostility to competing endgames.  
- **I Put a Spell on You** — McCarran monorail bomb plot; Captain Curtis is the Legion mole path.  
- **Things That Go Boom** — NCR asks you to secure **Boomer** support / munitions value for the war (Boomers at Nellis — not a Legion monorail quest).  
- **Arizona Killer** — Kimball assassination path exists on **anti-NCR** banners (Legion / other); not an NCR loyalty mission.

## Legion path — Render Unto Caesar (and related)

**Hook:** Answer the bull after the Fort invitation.  
**Giver / place:** The Fort; Caesar; frumentarii (Vulpes often early at Nipton).  
**Objectives (pattern):**
1. Reach the Fort (Cottonwood Cove ferry / Cursor Lucullus) and survive audience rules.  
2. Complete Caesar’s favor chain (**Render Unto Caesar**) — undermine NCR and Strip independence as ordered.  
3. Optional/related: **Et Tumor, Brute?** — medical crisis around Caesar’s tumor (treat, ignore, or exploit).  
4. Field ops may include Nelson (**We Are Legion**), Kimball hit paths, and anti-NCR sabotage.  
5. Fight the second battle of Hoover Dam for the Legion.  
**Finale container:** **Veni, Vidi, Vici**.  
**Notes:** Extreme companion and town reactions. Many NCR quests fail permanently.

## Endgame battle quests (path-dependent titles)

**Hook:** Second Battle of Hoover Dam — four major political endings.  
**Shared objective skeleton:**
1. Commit to a final banner (or Independent).  
2. Complete last ally call-ins if unlocked (Boomers artillery, Brotherhood, Khans, Enclave remnants, etc.).  
3. Fight or command dam set pieces.  
4. Resolve Legate Lanius / command-post beats per path.  
5. Epilogue reflects prior town and faction states.  
**Named finales:**
- **Eureka!** — NCR  
- **Veni, Vidi, Vici** — Legion  
- **All or Nothing** — House  
- **No Gods, No Masters** — Independent / Yes Man

## Main-spine courier tips (OVERSEER)
- Strip credit (~2,000 caps classically) or fame is a soft gate — plan early.  
- Platinum Chip is the plot battery — if stuck, reread active Pip-Boy steps.  
- You can soft-play multiple factions until assassination / Don’t Tread / House-kill locks fire.  
- Save before Ring-a-Ding-Ding and before any “kill this leader” prompt.  
- **They Went That-A-Way** route memory aid: Primm → Nipton → Novac → (Boulder City) → Strip.
