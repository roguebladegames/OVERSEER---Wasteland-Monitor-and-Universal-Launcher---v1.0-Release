# OVERSEER Quest Codex — Side Operations (Base Game)

Original briefings. Grouped by region. Objectives checked against real quest roles (not a GPS walkthrough).

---

## Goodsprings / I-15 south

### Ghost Town Gunfight
**Hook:** Joe Cobb’s Powder Gangers want Ringo; Goodsprings must fight or fold.  
**Giver / place:** Ringo (hiding at Doc Mitchell’s) / town defense.  
**Objectives:**
1. Decide to defend Goodsprings (this quest) rather than aid Cobb.  
2. Recruit helpers — Sunny Smiles, Trudy, Chet (supplies), Easy Pete (dynamite if skill/reputation allows), others as available.  
3. Arm/train townsfolk as the quest steps require.  
4. Win the schoolhouse street fight.  
**Notes:** Early reputation swing. Opposite quest is **Run Goodsprings Run**.

### Run Goodsprings Run
**Hook:** Side with Joe Cobb against the town.  
**Objectives:** Help the Powder Ganger plan; assault/raid Goodsprings; complete Cobb’s outcome.  
**Notes:** Burns Goodsprings bridges (Sunny, Trudy, Doc’s town services/reputation).

### By a Campfire on the Trail
**See main quest codex** — Sunny’s gecko hunt + Benny tip (main spine, not a random chore).

## Primm / NCRCF

### My Kind of Town
**Hook:** Primm needs a sheriff after convict chaos.  
**Giver / place:** Primm (Beagle / town leadership threads).  
**Objectives:**
1. Resolve the law vacuum.  
2. Install a sheriff option: **Primm Slim** (robot), **NCR** (Lt. Hayes path), **Meyers** (independent ex-con), or related outcomes your version allows.  
3. Stabilize town services after installation.  
**Notes:** Choice permanently colors Primm’s flavor and some services.

### Emergency Radio
**Hook:** NCR emergency broadcast / prison-break neighborhood fallout tools.  
**Objectives:** Use or complete the emergency radio thread as Pip-Boy steps direct (ties Primm/NCRCF region support into NCR systems).  
**Notes:** Distinct from Keep Your Eyes on the Prize (Nipton recon).

### NCRCF / Powder Ganger leadership work
**Hook:** Eddie’s gang holds the correctional facility.  
**Objectives:** Confront or deal with Powder Ganger leadership at NCRCF; optional Vault 19 / Samuel Cooke split later.  
**Notes:** Joe Cobb is the Goodsprings arm; Eddie is the prison boss — do not swap them.

## Mojave Outpost / south road

### Keep Your Eyes on the Prize
**Hook:** Nipton has gone silent.  
**Giver / place:** Ranger Ghost, Mojave Outpost.  
**Objectives:**
1. Investigate Nipton.  
2. Learn the Legion destroyed the town (lottery aftermath).  
3. Report back for NCR fame/intel credit.  
**Notes:** This is recon, not “random outpost chores.”

### Can You Find It in Your Heart?
**Hook:** Personal favor for a soldier at the Outpost.  
**Giver / place:** Private Edwards (Mojave Outpost).  
**Objectives:** Resolve his personal request without wrecking post discipline.  
**Notes:** Small NCR rep / karma-style errand.

## Novac / east highway

### Come Fly With Me
**Hook:** Bright Brotherhood ghouls vs nightkin at REPCONN HQ.  
**Giver / place:** Novac tips → REPCONN test site east of Novac.  
**Objectives:**
1. Contact Jason Bright’s cult and Chris Haversam.  
2. Resolve Davison’s nightkin in the basement (speech, stealth, or combat).  
3. Optional: help or hinder Keely’s research cleanup.  
4. Complete launch prep / decide who “goes to the Great Journey.”  
**Notes:** Multi-path; heavy speech/sneak/combat variants.

### One for My Baby
**Hook:** Boone’s missing-wife noir in Novac.  
**Giver / place:** Craig Boone (dinosaur nest) after trust steps.  
**Objectives:**
1. Watch the motel with Boone at night / gather evidence as directed.  
2. Uncover Jeannie May Crawford’s role in the sale to Legion slavers.  
3. Decide how justice lands (Boone’s confrontation path).  
**Notes:** Unlocks deeper Boone companion trust toward **I Forgot to Remember to Forget**.

### The Screams of Brahmin
**Hook:** Unmarked rancher pest problem near Novac.  
**Objectives:** Stop whatever is killing brahmin; return for a small cap reward and Novac fame.  
**Notes:** Unmarked — easy to miss; not a major story quest.

## Boulder City

### Boulder City Showdown
**Hook:** NCR vs **Great Khans** hostage crisis after the Benny job (Jessup’s crew).  
**Giver / place:** Lieutenant Monroe / Boulder City ruins.  
**Objectives:** Negotiate release or open fire; resolve Khan prisoners vs NCR demands.  
**Notes:** **Not Powder Gangers.** Powder Gangers are Goodsprings/NCRCF.

## Freeside

### G.I. Blues
**Hook:** Kings vs NCR street friction in Freeside.  
**Giver / place:** The King / Pacer threads.  
**Objectives:** Mediate, escalate, or rearrange power so Freeside doesn’t explode; outcomes change Kings–NCR relations.  
**Notes:** Pacer is the hothead risk.

### Wang Dang Atomic Tango
**Hook:** Atomic Wrangler needs “talent.”  
**Giver / place:** James/Francine Garret, Atomic Wrangler.  
**Objectives:** Recruit three specialists (typically a protector, a seducer, and a “weird act” — e.g. bodyguard, prostitute, and special entertainer variants the quest accepts).  
**Notes:** Comedy vice quest; not Strip Chairmen business.

### Freeside Strip-access grind (not one formal quest name)
**Hook:** Need caps or fame for the Strip gate.  
**Objectives:** Earn ~2,000 caps classically and/or fame; Mick & Ralph pathways; Wrangler work; thug or saint methods.  
**Notes:** There is no single canonical quest titled “Debt Collector” for the gate — treat as economy/fame prep.

## The Strip

### How Little We Know
**Hook:** Omerta conspiracy inside Gomorrah.  
**Giver / place:** Cachino / Big Sal / Nero threads.  
**Objectives:**
1. Gain leverage (Cachino’s evidence path is the common clean route).  
2. Uncover or join the Omerta plot against the Strip.  
3. Resolve bosses — reform, replace, or bloodbath.  
**Notes:** High betrayal risk; save often. Can brick vendors if you massacre the family.

### Beyond the Beef
**Hook:** Missing persons behind Ultra-Luxe hospitality.  
**Giver / place:** Ultra-Luxe / Marjorie / Mortimer / Heck Gunderson threads.  
**Objectives:**
1. Investigate kitchens, members, and the White Glove secret.  
2. Choose humane exposure, blackmail, or embrace the horror.  
3. Resolve Heck Gunderson’s hostage/brahmin-baron pressure as steps require.  
**Notes:** Do not confuse with Westside **Bleed Me Dry** (The Family).

### Talent Pool
**Hook:** Tommy Torini needs acts for The Aces.  
**Giver / place:** The Tops, Aces theater.  
**Objectives:** Recruit billed performers the quest accepts; return to Torini.  
**Notes:** Chairmen entertainment side content — not Benny’s main plot.

### Bye Bye Love
**Hook:** Free trafficked workers tied to Omerta vice.  
**Giver / place:** Gomorrah / related Freeside hooks.  
**Objectives:** Extract targets cleanly or fail them; avoid collapsing the wrong hostages.  
**Notes:** Omerta-adjacent; pairs thematically with How Little We Know.

### The Moon Comes Over the Tower
**Hook:** Followers want a bug on House’s systems.  
**Giver / place:** Emily Ortal on the Strip (often after Lucky 38 / House contact).  
**Objectives:**
1. Accept Emily’s request (optional Barter for med supplies).  
2. Plant her bug on the designated Lucky 38 terminal.  
3. Return with success.  
**Notes:** Short Followers intel quest — **not** a Fort radio project and not HELIOS.

## Westside / North Vegas

### The White Wash
**Hook:** NCR sharecrop water is being stolen for Westside.  
**Giver / place:** NCR / Anderson / Westside co-op threads.  
**Objectives:** Trace the diversion; choose justice that may starve Westside or expose NCR/Followers-adjacent actors.  
**Notes:** Civic morality quest — not Ultra-Luxe.

### Someone to Watch Over Me
**Hook:** Westside needs real patrols, not only protection theater.  
**Objectives:** Help the local organizer stabilize neighborhood defense against Fiends/raiders.  
**Notes:** Community survival, not Strip fame.

### Bleed Me Dry
**Hook:** The Family needs blood resources.  
**Giver / place:** Vance / Westside sewers Family.  
**Objectives:** Supply blood packs / “livestock” logistics as quest steps demand; stay in their code.  
**Notes:** Sewer blood society — **not** White Gloves.

## NCR hubs (McCarran, Forlorn Hope, Camp Golf, etc.)

### I Put a Spell on You
**Hook:** Sabotage threat to Camp McCarran’s monorail.  
**Giver / place:** Colonel Hsu / Lt. Boyd / base security.  
**Objectives:** Investigate the mole plot; Captain Curtis is the Legion agent path; protect or betray the monorail per faction lean.  
**Notes:** Major NCR–Legion flashpoint inside the base.

### Three-Card Bounty
**Hook:** Standing bounties on Fiend leaders.  
**Giver / place:** Major Dhatri, Camp McCarran.  
**Objectives:** Kill and turn in proof (heads preferably intact) for:
1. **Cook-Cook**  
2. **Driver Nephi**  
3. **Violet**  
**Notes:** **Motor-Runner** is the Vault 3 Fiend boss — separate from these three bounties.

### Hard Luck Blues
**Hook:** Vault 34 disaster spillover / contaminated crisis under NCR watch.  
**Objectives:** Investigate Vault 34 / irradiated survivor problem; rescue or write off per ethics; secure related systems as Pip-Boy steps show.  
**Notes:** Vault combat + civilian triage.

### Medical Mystery
**Hook:** Clinic diagnostic errand.  
**Giver / place:** New Vegas Medical Clinic sphere.  
**Objectives:** Fetch/diagnose as the doctor’s steps require.  
**Notes:** Small medical side content.

### No, Not Much
**Hook:** Camp Golf / Bitter Springs support package.  
**Giver / place:** Camp Golf officers (ties to Hanlon’s network).  
**Objectives:** Complete the bundled NCR favors — includes threads like infirmary help and **Climb Ev'ry Mountain** (see below).  
**Notes:** Quiet military checklist, not a dam finale.

### Climb Ev'ry Mountain
**Hook:** Someone is killing Bitter Springs refugees.  
**Giver / place:** Captain Gilles, **Bitter Springs** (NCR camp) — **not** Red Rock chem lab.  
**Objectives:**
1. Track the attacker to the Great Khan supply caves near Bitter Springs.  
2. Deal with **Oscar Velasco** (speech surrender or combat).  
3. Report to Gilles; handle supply cache fallout.  
**Notes:** NCR quest about a rogue Khan — do not file this as Papa Khan’s chem story.

### Restoring Hope
**Hook:** Camp Forlorn Hope is collapsing under Legion pressure.  
**Objectives:** Stabilize via medical, ammo, armor, and morale tasks officers assign.  
**Notes:** East front logistics package.

### Return to Sender
**Hook:** Ranger station data may be falsified.  
**Giver / place:** Chief Hanlon / Camp Golf → ranger stations.  
**Objectives:** Visit ranger stations; uncover the deception; choose expose Hanlon’s lie or bury it.  
**Notes:** Major NCR conscience quest.

### Flags of Our Foul-Ups
**Hook:** Camp Golf’s worst squad needs training.  
**Objectives:** Drill Misfits into competence (or fail comically).  
**Notes:** Hanlon/Golf flavor.

### Eye for an Eye
**Hook:** Retaliation strike tied to Legion atrocities / Forlorn Hope pressure.  
**Objectives:** Hit assigned Legion targets; mind civilian cost.  
**Notes:** NCR offensive side op.

### There Stands the Grass
**Hook:** OSI wants Vault 22 data.  
**Giver / place:** Thomas Hildern, Camp McCarran OSI.  
**Objectives:** Enter Vault 22 (spore horror); retrieve data; decide how much truth Hildern gets vs Angela Williams’ ethics.  
**Notes:** Science morality — Followers are not the quest giver (OSI is NCR).

### That Lucky Old Sun
**Hook:** HELIOS One power plant control.  
**Giver / place:** Fantastic / Ignacio Rivas / plant systems.  
**Objectives:** Repair/route HELIOS; choose power beneficiary (NCR, Full power to region variants, House-related options, or destructive overload paths).  
**Notes:** Who gets power changes politics. Includes **Ant Misbehavin'** pest sub-work when ants infest plant systems.

### Ant Misbehavin'
**Hook:** Ant infestation in HELIOS systems.  
**Objectives:** Clear ants so plant work can continue.  
**Notes:** Often paired with That Lucky Old Sun.

## Brotherhood of Steel (Hidden Valley)

### Still in the Dark
**Hook:** Main Mojave Brotherhood chapter questline.  
**Giver / place:** Hidden Valley bunker (Elder McNamara / patrol chain).  
**Objectives:** Gain bunker trust; complete recon and chapter tasks; navigate lockdown paranoia; set up the Hardin vs McNamara leadership fork.  
**Notes:** Surface entrance is easy to miss; Veronica helps find the chapter.

### Tend to Your Business
**Hook:** Internal chapter discipline/business McNamara assigns.  
**Objectives:** Resolve the assigned internal problem (crime/heresy/patrol issue as scripted).  
**Notes:** Side branch inside the bunker politics.

### Eyesight to the Blind
**Hook:** Brotherhood recon/tech task in the McNamara chain.  
**Objectives:** Complete the field objective the elder/patrol board assigns (scout/secure as Pip-Boy states).  
**Notes:** BoS chapter work — not Van Graff shopping.

## Jacobstown / Black Mountain

### Guess Who I Saw Today
**Hook:** Dr. Henry’s nightkin Stealth Boy research.  
**Giver / place:** Jacobstown (Dr. Henry; Lily involved).  
**Objectives:** Help gather what Henry needs; manage nightkin test ethics; decide how far the project goes.  
**Notes:** Ties into Lily’s medication care as companion content.

### Unfriendly Persuasion
**Hook:** Mercenaries plan to attack Jacobstown.  
**Objectives:** Defuse or destroy the attack plan before the lodge burns.  
**Notes:** Protect Marcus’s peace project.

### Crazy, Crazy, Crazy
**Hook:** Tabitha’s radio tyranny on Black Mountain.  
**Objectives:** Ascend Black Mountain; deal with Tabitha and Rhonda outcome; free Raul if still held.  
**Notes:** Not Jacobstown; not REPCONN Davison.

## Great Khans (Red Rock)

### Oh My Papa
**Hook:** Papa Khan’s political future after NCR trauma and Legion temptation.  
**Giver / place:** Red Rock Canyon.  
**Objectives:** Steer tribe toward Legion alliance, independence, or other scripted futures via Regis/Papa speech chains.  
**Notes:** Endgame ally call-in can depend on this relationship.

### Aba Daba Honeymoon
**Hook:** Red Rock drug lab needs chem running.  
**Giver / place:** **Diane** at the Red Rock drug lab (Great Khans) — **not** a Strip tourist quest.  
**Objectives:** Run Diane’s chem delivery jobs (multiple packages/destinations as steps list); survive Fiend turf legs.  
**Notes:** Khan chem economy. Was previously miscategorized under Strip in older OVERSEER notes.

### Young Hearts
**Hook:** Cross-faction lovers (Khan / NCR entanglement).  
**Objectives:** Help the couple’s plan without accidentally sparking a massacre.  
**Notes:** Red Rock social side quest.

## Boomers (Nellis)

### Volare!
**Hook:** Earn Boomer trust past the anti-air curtain and unlock their story payoff.  
**Giver / place:** Nellis AFB (Pearl / Loyal after you survive approach).  
**Objectives:**
1. Reach Nellis alive (or carefully) under flak.  
2. Complete loyalty tasks (medical, morale, technical).  
3. Progress Loyal’s bomber dream (parts/repairs as quest states).  
4. Unlock Boomer friendship / endgame artillery support.  
**Notes:** Pairs with reputation grind.

### Why Can't We Be Friends?
**Hook:** Reputation container toward Boomer idolization.  
**Objectives:** Keep completing Boomer-positive actions until reputation thresholds unlock full cooperation.  
**Notes:** Often progresses alongside Volare!

## Side-quest operating rules (OVERSEER)
1. Pip-Boy active steps beat this codex if they disagree.  
2. Speech/barter/sneak often replace corpses.  
3. Town sheriff and family resolutions permanently color services.  
4. Named hard save before faction assassination prompts.  
5. If a title was ever filed under the wrong region in old notes, trust **giver + place** above vibe.

*End side quest codex (accuracy pass).*
