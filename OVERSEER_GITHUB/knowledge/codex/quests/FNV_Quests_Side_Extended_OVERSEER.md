# OVERSEER Quest Codex — Extended Side Index

Additional base-game side operations in compressed OVERSEER form. Use with `FNV_Quests_Side_OVERSEER.md`. Accuracy-focused wording.

## NCR military & rangers

### Don’t Tread on the Bear!
**Place:** Late NCR commitment.  
**Objectives:** Accept NCR exclusive endgame demands; other banners (House/Legion/Independent) lock hostile.  
**Notes:** Not the same quest as Things That Go Boom.

### Things That Go Boom
**Place:** NCR leadership / embassy-military chain.  
**Objectives:** Secure Boomer cooperation / munitions value for the Republic’s war plan (Nellis).  
**Notes:** Boomers quest pressure from the NCR side — not the McCarran monorail mole plot (that is I Put a Spell on You).

### Return to Sender
**Place:** Camp Golf → ranger stations.  
**Objectives:** Audit station reports; uncover falsified data; expose or protect Chief Hanlon.

### Restoring Hope
**Place:** Camp Forlorn Hope.  
**Objectives:** Medical, ammo, armor, morale deliveries/tasks for the east front.

### No, Not Much
**Place:** Camp Golf / Bitter Springs package.  
**Objectives:** Complete the bundled officer favors (includes links to infirmary work and Climb Ev'ry Mountain).

### Climb Ev'ry Mountain
**Place:** Bitter Springs (Captain Gilles) → Great Khan supply caves.  
**Objectives:** Stop Oscar Velasco’s attacks on refugees; speech or combat; return to Gilles.  
**Notes:** NCR quest — not Red Rock’s Aba Daba chem runs.

### I Don’t Hurt Anymore
**Place:** Near NCR medical/social NPCs.  
**Objectives:** Resolve the personal medical-social problem the giver scripts.

### Three-Card Bounty
**Place:** Major Dhatri, Camp McCarran.  
**Objectives:** Heads of Cook-Cook, Driver Nephi, and Violet.  
**Notes:** Motor-Runner = Vault 3, separate.

### I Put a Spell on You
**Place:** Camp McCarran.  
**Objectives:** Monorail bomb investigation; Curtis mole path; protect or sabotage per lean.

### Flags of Our Foul-Ups
**Place:** Camp Golf.  
**Objectives:** Train the Misfits.

### Eye for an Eye
**Place:** Forlorn Hope offensive package.  
**Objectives:** Strike assigned Legion targets.

### Pressing Matters
**Place:** NCR industrial/pressure side content as marked in Pip-Boy.  
**Objectives:** Complete the facility/pressure task given (follow active steps).

### We Will All Go Together
**Place:** NCR memorial/funeral-related honor task as marked.  
**Objectives:** Complete the remembrance/recovery steps officers assign.

## Mutual exclusion warnings (OVERSEER)
- Hard Legion leadership loyalty fails many NCR lines.  
- Don’t Tread on the Bear! freezes non-NCR endings.  
- Killing Mr. House early collapses House Always Wins into Wild Card or others.  
- Brotherhood civil outcome changes shop/patrol access.  
- Omerta/White Glove massacres can remove vendors permanently.  
- Three-Card Bounty targets ≠ Motor-Runner (Vault 3).  
- Climb Ev'ry Mountain is Bitter Springs NCR — not Red Rock chem lab.  
- Aba Daba Honeymoon is Diane’s Red Rock chem runs — not Strip tourism.

When in doubt: **named hard save** before faction assassination prompts.
