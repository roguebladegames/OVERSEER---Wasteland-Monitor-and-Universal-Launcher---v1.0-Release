# OVERSEER Faction Briefings — Mojave Power Map

Original operational faction dossiers for OVERSEER RAG. Each entry is self-contained: **who they are**, **where they are on the map**, **who speaks for them**, **which quests matter**, and **what they are not**.

Location fields (use these when the Courier asks “where is / where are”):
- **Primary HQ** — main base or capital
- **Locations** — all important sites this faction holds or uses
- **Map region** — rough Mojave orientation

Not wiki paste. Rescan docs after edits.

## New California Republic (NCR)

**Scope:** Base Mojave  
**Role:** Expansionist republic occupying the Mojave with troops, taxes, and bureaucracy. Main western superpower opposite Caesar’s Legion.  
**Primary HQ:** Camp McCarran (southwest of the Strip — main Mojave air/logistics command under Colonel Hsu).  
**Locations:**
- Camp McCarran — troop hub, monorail, Fiend pressure, OSI offices
- Hoover Dam — strategic prize; Colonel Moore / General Oliver late-game presence
- NCR Embassy — on the Strip (Ambassador Crocker)
- Camp Forlorn Hope — eastern front vs Legion
- Camp Golf — NCR Ranger HQ (Chief Hanlon)
- Mojave Outpost — south highway gate (Rangers Ghost/Jackson, Major Knight)
- Sharecrop farms — food production under NCR occupation
- Ranger stations — chain across the map (Return to Sender network)
- Primm / other towns when NCR law is installed
**Map region:** Central and southern Mojave occupation belt; east front at Forlorn Hope; dam on the Colorado.  
**Key NPCs:** President Aaron Kimball; Ambassador Crocker; Colonel James Hsu; Colonel Cassandra Moore; General Lee Oliver; Chief Hanlon; Major Dhatri; Lieutenant Carrie Boyd; Captain Curtis; Lieutenant Hayes; Rangers Ghost and Jackson. Companion with NCR scars: Craig Boone (Novac — not command staff).  
**Major quests:** For the Republic, Part 2; I Put a Spell on You; Three-Card Bounty (Cook-Cook, Driver Nephi, Violet); Restoring Hope; Return to Sender; Flags of Our Foul-Ups; Things That Go Boom (Boomer support ask); Don’t Tread on the Bear!; Climb Ev'ry Mountain (Bitter Springs); finale Eureka!  
**Wants:** Hold Hoover Dam, tax trade, push Legion back, dominate or annex New Vegas.  
**Strengths:** Manpower, logistics, state structure, rangers.  
**Weaknesses:** Overstretch, corruption, unpopular taxes, Fiend pressure.  
**Not:** Followers, Strip families, or Brotherhood.

## Caesar's Legion

**Scope:** Base Mojave  
**Role:** Slaver military empire under Caesar; conquest culture east of the Colorado with west-bank footholds.  
**Primary HQ:** The Fort (east of the Colorado River — Caesar’s tent city capital).  
**Locations:**
- The Fort — Legion capital; Caesar, Lucius, arena, praetorians
- Cottonwood Cove — west-bank port and slave camp (Aurelius of Phoenix; ferry via Cursor Lucullus)
- Nelson — captured NCR town (Dead Sea)
- Nipton — early terror showcase (Vulpes Inculta encounter site, not a permanent capital)
- Legion raid camps and road patrols across eastern/southern Mojave approaches
- Hoover Dam — endgame objective (Legate Lanius)
**Map region:** East bank capital; west footholds at Cottonwood Cove and Nelson; terror reach into Nipton and raiding roads.  
**Key NPCs:** Caesar; Legate Lanius; Vulpes Inculta; Lucius; Aurelius of Phoenix; Dead Sea; Cursor Lucullus; Antony; Canyon Runner; Karl; Silus (captive at McCarran).  
**Major quests:** Render Unto Caesar; Nipton intro; Cold, Cold Heart; We Are Legion; Arizona Killer; finale Veni, Vidi, Vici.  
**Wants:** Break NCR at the dam; end Vegas independence; enforce Legion law.  
**Not:** Powder Gangers, Fiends, or White Legs (Zion).

## Mr. House (Strip authority)

**Scope:** Base Mojave  
**Role:** Pre-war autocrat mind running New Vegas from the Lucky 38 via Securitrons.  
**Primary HQ:** Lucky 38 penthouse (The Strip).  
**Locations:**
- Lucky 38 — House’s body and control room; penthouse audience
- The Strip corridor — Securitron-policed tourist casinos
- Strip north gate — credit/fame checkpoint
- Underground Securitron vault / bunker systems (platinum chip upgrade chain under the metro)
- Victor patrol points — Goodsprings welcome and Mojave field presence
**Map region:** Absolute center — New Vegas Strip and its robot infrastructure.  
**Key NPCs:** Mr. House; Jane (floor Securitron); Victor; Emily Ortal. Casino families are separate factions under Strip rules.  
**Major quests:** The House Always Wins; The Moon Comes Over the Tower; dam finale All or Nothing.  
**Not:** Yes Man independent path. Not the three casino families as identical to House.

## Yes Man / Independent (Wild Card)

**Scope:** Base Mojave  
**Role:** Courier hijack of Strip OS after removing House — not a starting nation with its own towns.  
**Primary HQ:** Lucky 38 (becomes Courier HQ once House is out).  
**Locations:**
- The Tops — Yes Man first secured via Benny’s tools / related plot beats
- Lucky 38 — independent command deck
- Same Securitron vault/bunker upgrade path as House
- Strip gate and streets once bots answer to you
- Side Bet sites across the Mojave (whatever regions you stabilize before the dam)
**Map region:** Strip-centered; authority projected only as far as your prep and robots reach.  
**Key NPCs:** Yes Man; Victor as infrastructure; Benny as early catalyst.  
**Major quests:** Wild Card / Ace in the Hole; Change in Management; You and What Army?; Side Bets / Finishing Touches; finale No Gods, No Masters.  
**Not:** A pre-built army with public forts like McCarran or the Fort.

**Role:** Gated tourist zone under Securitron law — political prize, not one army.

**Locations:**

- Lucky 38
- The Tops (Chairmen)
- Gomorrah (Omertas)
- Ultra-Luxe (White Glove Society)

**Map region:** Center of New Vegas; Freeside wraps the outside of the gate.  
**Major quests:** Ring-a-Ding-Ding!; How Little We Know; Beyond the Beef; Talent Pool; Bye Bye Love; The Moon Comes Over the Tower (Emily Ortal bug job). Aba Daba Honeymoon is a Great Khan chem quest at Red Rock — not Strip tourism. Strip access is caps/fame at the gate, not a single named “debt collector” quest.  
**Not:** Freeside. Not Westside.

## Brotherhood of Steel (Mojave chapter)

**Scope:** Base Mojave  
**Role:** Isolationist tech-knight chapter in a hidden bunker.  
**Primary HQ:** Hidden Valley bunker (underground bunker complex south/southeast of the Strip grid — bunker doors in the Hidden Valley bunkers).  
**Locations:**
- Hidden Valley — main bunker (McNamara, Hardin, Ramos, Lorenzo)
- HELIOS One — historical scar and power-plant flashpoint (That Lucky Old Sun)
- 188 Trading Post area — common Veronica meetup on the surface

**Map region:** Hidden Valley south of greater Vegas; HELIOS east-southeast.  
**Key NPCs:** Elder Nolan McNamara; Head Paladin Hardin; Paladin Ramos; Knight Lorenzo; Veronica Santangelo. Elijah is former elder (expansion antagonist later).  
**Major quests:** Still in the Dark; Tend to Your Business; Eyesight to the Blind; I Could Make You Care; HELIOS interaction.  
**Not:** Enclave remnants. Not Van Graffs. Not Think Tank.
