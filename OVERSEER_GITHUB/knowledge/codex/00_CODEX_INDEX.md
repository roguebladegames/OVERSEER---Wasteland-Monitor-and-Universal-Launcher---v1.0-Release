# OVERSEER Codex — Index

**Classification:** Local knowledge for OVERSEER only  
**Authorship:** Newly written for OVERSEER. Not transcribed from wikis, manuals, or official strategy guides.  
**Purpose:** Give the terminal original, practical briefings on quests, people, and power blocs so chat advice stays grounded without lifting third-party prose.

## How to use
1. Drop stays under `knowledge/codex/`.
2. Hit **Rescan Docs** in OVERSEER after edits.
3. Ask Overseer about a quest, person, or faction by name; retrieval pulls these chunks.

| Folder | Contents |

| `quests/` | Story and side operations with objectives in OVERSEER voice |

## Design rules (for future authors)
- Rewrite every explanation from first principles; never paste external articles.
- Prefer actionable objectives over story essays.
- Mark branching outcomes without spoiling every line of dialogue.
- When unsure of an obscure name, say so rather than inventing.
