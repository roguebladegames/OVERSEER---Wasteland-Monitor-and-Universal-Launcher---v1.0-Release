# OVERSEER NPC Dossiers — Mojave Persons of Interest

Original operational dossiers for OVERSEER RAG. Functional identity only: who they are, where they live, what they do for the Courier, and what they are **not**. Not wiki biography paste.

**Rules for this file**
- One **named person per row** (no slash-bundles of different people).
- Role text must stand alone if retrieved alone.
- Always state: **location**, **function**, **base vs DLC**, **companion or not**, key quests by name when known.
- Avoid DLC keywords in base-game rows unless the character is DLC-only (chunker treats those words as DLC scope).

Coverage: plot-critical, companions, faction leads, town fixtures, notable antagonists. Unnamed settlers omitted.
